About

Hi, I’m Prajwal.

I build data systems and use them to help people make decisions.

My first years in analytics were in Bengaluru, where I worked with healthcare claims and electronic health records to answer research questions. At DP World, I worked on a different puzzle: how to piece together a container’s journey when some of the timestamps are missing.

In Dubai, I helped build a business intelligence function for a group with eight affiliate businesses. That work meant bringing data from different systems together and agreeing on what a number should mean before putting it on a dashboard.

One question runs through all of it: what will someone do differently once they have this information?
